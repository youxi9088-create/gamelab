How to climb

Find the key somewhere on the tower, then reach the gate at the top.
Lava rises from below. Gold and chests fill your greed: more score, faster lava.
Dark doorways lead through the tower to its far side. Treasure waits there.
Slash skeletons, rats, bats and spiders. Dragons can't be slain, but your sword can parry their fireballs.
Press down in the air to ground-pound. The higher you fall, the bigger the blast: foes fly, gold sprays loose. Land it on a guardian's weak spot for double damage.
Chain coins, kills and smashes into a combo. Staying in the air keeps it alive, and the multiplier boosts every point.
Hourglasses freeze the lava for a few seconds. Red crystal hearts heal.
Behind the gate a guardian waits. Dodge the glowing warnings, strike its glowing weak spots after it attacks, and parry its fireballs straight back at it.
In the guardian's hall, stand at a cannon and press the sword button to fire it. Grab bombs from the crates and throw them. Jump on the floating platforms to dodge.
Keyboard: A/D or arrows to move, Space or W to jump, J or X to swing, S to drop down (in the air: ground-pound), E to enter a door, Esc to pause.
