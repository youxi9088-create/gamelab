Pile-Up Poker

  • You'll get 5 cards each round. Place 4 on the grid and discard one.
  • Try to form poker hands in every row, column and in the four corners.
  • Score all nine hands on the grid and your discard pile becomes a hand too.

Better hands earn more points. Corners pay double, your discards pay triple, and every extra hand you make pumps up your multiplier.
